Common questions

How do I use scientific functions?

Tap SCI above the keypad. Use RAD or DEG to switch between radians and degrees for trigonometric calculations.

How do I reuse a previous calculation?

Tap an expression in the ledger to bring it back to the display. Tap a result to insert it into the current expression.

How do I remove calculation history?

Tap the remove button beside an entry to delete it, or tap CLEAR in the ledger to remove all entries. Cleared entries cannot be restored from within the app.

Privacy Policy

Ledger 63 does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data, analytics, or tracking information. Your calculation history is stored solely on your device using local iOS storage (UserDefaults) and can be cleared at any time within the app. Read the full Privacy Policy.
