Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

In short

Ledger 63 does not collect, transmit, track, or sell any personal data. All calculations and history stay locally on your device.

Data Collection and Storage

Ledger 63 operates entirely offline on your iPhone:

  • No Personal Data Collected: We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, device identifiers, or usage analytics.
  • Local Calculation History: Your calculation ledger history is stored strictly on your device using standard iOS local storage (UserDefaults) so you can view and reuse past calculations. You can delete individual entries or clear all history at any time inside the app.
  • No Third-Party SDKs or Ads: Ledger 63 contains no third-party analytics, advertising networks, or tracking scripts.

Support Communications

If you choose to email us for support at gnemo8687@gmail.com, we will only use your email address and the information you provide to respond to your inquiry.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this Privacy Policy in future versions of Ledger 63, we will post the revised policy on this page and update the effective date above.
